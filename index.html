Why AcademiqOS? Beyond Centralized Cloud Lock-In.

Traditional commercial cloud SaaS systems (like standard web platforms) force schools to surrender student data, pay perpetual rent on external servers, and suffer under sluggish legacy architectures.

AcademiqOS flips this model entirely. Inspired by the GitHub Enterprise paradigm, you license our high-performance core engine and host it securely inside your own campus perimeter or chosen local hardware.
